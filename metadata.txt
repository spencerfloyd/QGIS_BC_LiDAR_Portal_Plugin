description=Batch download Lidar point clouds, DEMs, and DSMs from the Government of British Columbia LidarBC Open Data Portal by drawing one or more areas of interest.
about=This plugin allows users to draw one or more areas of interest on the map and batch download matching Lidar tiles, including LAZ point clouds, DEMs, and DSM rasters, from the Government of British Columbia LidarBC ArcGIS REST Feature Server. Downloads are processed sequentially and automatically organized into subfolders by product type and scale. The plugin also supports filtering duplicate tile versions by date, allowing users to download the latest, oldest, or all available versions.
